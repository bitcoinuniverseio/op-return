How it comes together

Four ideas. One connected experience.

Protocol details matter most when they help you understand what you are about to create, collect, or send.

01 / IDEA

The carrier

OP_RETURN stores provably unspendable data in a Bitcoin output.

02 / IDEA

The language

OP-20 recognizes deploy, mint, and transfer actions.

03 / IDEA

The identity

Tickers are compared without letter-case differences.

04 / IDEA

The view

Compatible services interpret OP-20 activity from confirmed transactions.

Keep the meaning clear

Bitcoin confirms the transaction. Compatible services reveal the experience.

OP-20 balances are interpreted by compatible services and are separate from Bitcoin’s native consensus rules. Always review the exact payload, recipient, and fees before signing.
